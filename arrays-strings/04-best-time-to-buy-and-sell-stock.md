# Problem: Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/best-time-to-buy-and-sell-stock/

## Approach

I kept track of the lowest price seen so far.
For each price, I calculated the profit and stored the maximum profit.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

## Notes

Learned how to find the maximum profit by keeping track of the minimum price.
