# Problem: Longest Common Prefix

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/longest-common-prefix/

## Approach

I compared the characters of all the strings one by one.
I stopped when the characters were different and stored the common part as the prefix.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n × m)
- Space: O(m)

## Notes

Learned how to compare multiple strings to find their common prefix.
