# Problem: Reverse Linked List

**Difficulty:** Easy

**Link:** https://leetcode.com/problems/reverse-linked-list/

## Approach

I used three pointers: previous, current, and nextNode.
I changed the links one by one to reverse the linked list.

## Complexity

- Time: O(n)
- Space: O(1)

## Notes

Learned how to reverse a linked list by changing the direction of the links.
